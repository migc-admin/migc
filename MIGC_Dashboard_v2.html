Sandbagger Watch
⚠️ Sandbagger score is purely statistical. It reflects handicap inflation patterns — upward recent trend, gap from personal best, volatility, and overall regression. No scoring differentials or actual round data available.
Player SB Score Tier Recent Trend Gap From Best Consistency Rtg Δ All-Time Current HCI Rounds
Methodology
The sandbagger score (0–100) is a composite of four signals:
Recent Trend (0–35 pts) — Average HCI of last 10 entries vs. prior 10 entries. Rising = suspicious.
Gap From Personal Best (0–35 pts) — How far current HCI sits above their lowest recorded index. Players at or near their best score 0.
Consistency Rating (0–20 pts) — Standard deviation across all HCI entries. Erratic swings inflate scores.
Overall Regression (0–10 pts) — Only fires when current HCI exceeds starting HCI. Steady improvers don't get penalized.
